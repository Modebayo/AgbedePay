The Flowband — construction

  • Money in motion — a single unbroken band: funds flow without friction.
  • The A counter — rising and returning legs form the letter through negative space.
  • Movement — the rounded apex fold: money pivoting to grow.
  • Connection — the bridge bar links person to business, payer to payee.
  • Growth — the rising wing lifts toward the settlement node.
  • Settlement node — the completed transaction; green in colour.

Built on a strict 100 × 100 grid with 11-unit strokes and rounded joins — it holds its shape from a 16 px favicon to a building sign.

The gradient runs with the flow of money — navy (trust) → blue (technology) → green (growth). The symbol never depends on colour: black and white versions carry the same idea in a single ink.
